## 📌 Project Overview
This project goes beyond a static biography by organizing information into scannable visual modules and interactive data-driven components. Visitors can explore Elon Musk’s evolution from age 13 to present day, dive into his companies' breakthrough moments and near-death experiences, and examine the core philosophies driving his work.

---

## ✨ Features & Rubric Requirements Met

### 1. Introduction & Profile
- Displays his full name, official portrait, and a clear executive summary highlighting his first-principles philosophy and high-risk engineering ventures.

### 2. Interactive Age & Wealth Chronology (Milestones)
- Recreates the iconic visual milestone card layout:
  - Spans key turning points across ten distinct stages: **1984 (Age 13), 1999 (Age 28), 2002 (Age 31), 2011 (Age 40), 2014 (Age 43), 2016 (Age 45), 2018 (Age 47), 2020 (Age 49), 2022 (Age 51), and 2025 (Age 52/53)**.
  - **Dynamic Modal / Drawer Interaction:** Clicking any card opens detailed insights covering what he built, critical hurdles faced, and net worth context at that age.

### 3. Ventures Matrix: The Rise & The Fall
- Highlights five landmark companies and ventures:
  - **SpaceX** (Commercial orbital flight, Starship & Starlink)
  - **Tesla, Inc.** (Electric mobility, Megapack storage & autonomous robotics)
  - **Neuralink** (Brain-computer interfaces & clinical neuro-implants)
  - **xAI** (Frontier reasoning AI & Grok)
  - **X (Twitter)** & **The Boring Company**
- Features an interactive switcher/tab system detailing his role, official external links, the **"Rise"** (breakthrough achievements), and the **"Fall"** (existential crises like SpaceX's 2008 launch failures and Tesla's 2018 production hell).

### 4. Impact, Leadership Lessons & Takeaways
- **Key Contributions:** Reusable orbital spaceflight commercialization and worldwide automotive fleet electrification.
- **Actionable Takeaways:** First-principles deconstruction over analogy and high-frequency hardware prototyping.

### 5. Technical Architecture & Data-Driven Rendering
- Follows the single-file structure (`index.html`) using vanilla JavaScript, CSS custom variables, and semantic HTML5.
- Milestone and venture sections are rendered dynamically from structured JavaScript arrays (`timelineData`, `venturesData`), demonstrating clean code separation without unnecessary external dependencies.
