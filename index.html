Impact, Takeaways & Sources

Meaningful achievements

  • Reusable orbital rockets: SpaceX landed and re-flew orbital-class boosters, sharply cutting launch costs.
  • Electrifying cars: Tesla pushed the whole auto industry toward EVs and battery supply chains.

Lessons to take away

  • First-principles thinking: break problems down to physical truths (e.g. raw material cost) and rebuild from zero.
  • Iterate over over-plan: build, test, break and learn from real telemetry, even if prototypes fail in public.
